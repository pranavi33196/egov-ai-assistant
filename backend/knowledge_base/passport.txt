SERVICE: Passport
DESCRIPTION: An official document issued by a government, certifying the holder's identity and citizenship.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Indian citizens.
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Address proof, Date of Birth proof, non-ECR proof if applicable.
APPLICATION PROCESS: Register on Passport Seva portal, pay fees, book appointment, visit PSK.
IMPORTANT NOTES: Requirements and procedures may vary by state/UT. Please verify the latest information through the relevant official government portal.
