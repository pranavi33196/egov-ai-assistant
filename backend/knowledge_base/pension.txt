SERVICE: Pension
DESCRIPTION: Financial assistance provided to senior citizens, widows, and disabled persons.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Varies based on pension type (e.g., above 60 for Old Age Pension).
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Age proof, Income Certificate, Aadhaar, Bank passbook.
APPLICATION PROCESS: Apply through state social welfare portal or local panchayat/municipal office.
IMPORTANT NOTES: Requirements and procedures may vary by state/UT. Please verify the latest information through the relevant official government portal.
