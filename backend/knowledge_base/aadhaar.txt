SERVICE: Aadhaar Unique Identification (UIDAI)
DESCRIPTION: Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identity number issued by UIDAI to all residents of India based on biometric and demographic data. It serves as universal proof of identity and address across India.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Any individual residing in India for at least 182 days in the preceding 12 months, including newborns and minors.
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Proof of Identity (POI - Passport, PAN, Voter ID, Ration Card), Proof of Address (POA - Electricity bill, Water bill, Bank passbook), Date of Birth Proof (Birth certificate, SSLC book).
APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Locate your nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra or Authorized Enrollment Center via uidai.gov.in.
2. Book an online appointment or visit directly with original verification documents.
3. Complete biometric capture (10 fingerprints, iris scan, facial photograph) and demographic data entry.
4. Collect the 28-digit Enrollment Slip / Acknowledgement ID (EID) with date-time stamp.
5. Download e-Aadhaar online from uidai.gov.in using OTP once generated (usually 15-30 days).
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://uidai.gov.in / https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in
HELPLINE: Toll-Free 1947 | Email: help@uidai.gov.in
FEES: Free for initial enrollment. Rs. 50 for demographic updates; Rs. 100 for biometric updates.
