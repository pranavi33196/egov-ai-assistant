SERVICE: Ration Card
DESCRIPTION: A document for the purchase of essential commodities from fair price shops.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Permanent residents of the state.
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Address proof, Identity Proof, Income Certificate, Photographs.
APPLICATION PROCESS: Submit application to local rationing office with documents.
IMPORTANT NOTES: Requirements and procedures may vary by state/UT. Please verify the latest information through the relevant official government portal.
