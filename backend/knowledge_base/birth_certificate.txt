SERVICE: Birth Certificate
DESCRIPTION: Official record of a person's birth.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Any person born in the jurisdiction.
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Hospital discharge slip, parent's identity proof.
APPLICATION PROCESS: Register birth within 21 days at the local municipal or panchayat office.
IMPORTANT NOTES: Requirements and procedures may vary by state/UT. Please verify the latest information through the relevant official government portal.
