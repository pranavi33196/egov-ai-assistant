SERVICE: Scholarship
DESCRIPTION: Financial aid awarded to students for further education.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Students enrolled in recognized institutions, meeting income criteria.
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Marksheets, Income Certificate, Caste Certificate (if applicable), Bank details.
APPLICATION PROCESS: Apply via National Scholarship Portal or State specific portal.
IMPORTANT NOTES: Requirements and procedures may vary by state/UT. Please verify the latest information through the relevant official government portal.
