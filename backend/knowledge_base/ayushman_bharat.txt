SERVICE: Ayushman Bharat - PMJAY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana)
DESCRIPTION: World's largest government-funded healthcare scheme providing health insurance coverage of up to Rs. 5,00,000 per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across empaneled public and private hospitals.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Families identified under the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) database, including BPL ration card holders, rural occupational categories, and urban vulnerable workers.
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar Card, Ration Card (or Family ID / Samagra ID), PM-JAY Letter / SECC Confirmation, Active Mobile Number.
APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Check eligibility on the official portal (beneficiary.nha.gov.in) using your mobile number or Aadhaar.
2. If eligible, generate an Ayushman Card (Golden Card) online or visit any empaneled hospital / CSC Common Service Center / Ayushman Mitra kiosk.
3. Complete e-KYC using Aadhaar OTP or Face Authentication.
4. Download the PVC or digital Ayushman Card and present it at any empaneled hospital for 100% cashless treatment.
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in / https://pmjay.gov.in
HELPLINE: Toll-Free 14555 / 1800-111-565
FEES: 100% Free medical coverage and card generation.
SOURCE / SOURCE TYPE: National Health Authority (NHA) / Ministry of Health & Family Welfare
