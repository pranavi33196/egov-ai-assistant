SERVICE: Income Certificate
DESCRIPTION: Document certifying the annual income of a person or family.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Any resident requiring proof of income.
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Salary slips, IT returns, Ration Card, Aadhaar.
APPLICATION PROCESS: Apply online via state e-district portal or at local Tahsildar office.
IMPORTANT NOTES: Requirements and procedures may vary by state/UT. Please verify the latest information through the relevant official government portal.
