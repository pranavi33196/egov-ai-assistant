SERVICE: Voter Identity Card (EPIC)
DESCRIPTION: Electoral Photo Identity Card (EPIC) issued by the Election Commission of India (ECI) to eligible citizens to vote in democratic elections and serve as official government photo identification.
GENERAL ELIGIBILITY: Indian citizen aged 18 years or above on the qualifying date (1st January, April, July, October) and ordinary resident of the polling area.
REQUIRED DOCUMENTS: Passport-size photograph, Proof of Age (Birth certificate, Aadhaar, PAN, School certificate), Proof of Ordinary Residence (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, Ration Card, Bank Passbook).
APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit the National Voters' Service Portal (voters.eci.gov.in) or download the Voter Helpline App (VHA).
2. Register/Login using your mobile number and select 'New Voter Registration (Form 6)'.
3. Enter state, district, assembly constituency, personal details, and upload photograph & address proof.
4. Submit form and note down the Reference Tracking ID.
5. Booth Level Officer (BLO) will conduct field verification. EPIC card will be dispatched via India Post Speed Post. Digital e-EPIC can be downloaded instantly once enrolled.
OFFICIAL PORTAL: https://voters.eci.gov.in / https://eci.gov.in
HELPLINE: Toll-Free 1950 (Election Commission Helpline)
FEES: Free of cost for new registration and first EPIC card.
SOURCE / SOURCE TYPE: Election Commission of India (ECI)
